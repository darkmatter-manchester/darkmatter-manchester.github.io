I'm interested in hearing from prospective PhD students looking for UK-funded positions through the University of Manchester or the UK research council (STFC), as well as those arriving with their own scholarship or planning to apply for funding from their home country or government. The routes differ, but in every case I look for the same thing: strong motivation, solid preparation, and a genuine interest in the questions the group works on.

Your own funding

If you intend to apply for your own funding, or know of a scholarship not listed here, I'd still be glad to hear from you. The entry process and academic standards are the same regardless of the funding source — what matters is the strength of the application and the fit with the group.
