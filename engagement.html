Public engagement

Beyond the lab.

Dark matter is one of the biggest open questions in science — and one of the most captivating ways into physics for people of all ages. We share our research widely, through public lectures, school visits, festivals, and freely available resources.

From primary-school assemblies to science festivals and evening lectures, the group is active in bringing the search for dark matter to audiences far beyond the laboratory. We believe the questions we work on — what is the Universe made of? what holds galaxies together? — belong to everyone, and we enjoy sharing both the science and the experience of doing research at the frontier.
