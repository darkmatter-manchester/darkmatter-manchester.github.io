<h2 class="section-label">Overview</h2>

If dark matter interacts with ordinary matter, it might be produced
directly in the proton–proton collisions of the Large Hadron Collider.
Collider searches probe the same underlying physics as direct detection,
but from a complementary direction — and constraints from one inform the
interpretation of the other.

The group works on the ATLAS experiment at the LHC, connecting past and
ongoing collider searches for dark-sector and beyond-Standard-Model physics
to its direct-detection and phenomenology programme.

<h2 class="section-label">Current activities</h2>

Current activities include relating collider constraints to
direct-detection phenomenology, and the development and deployment of AI and
machine-learning techniques in analysis.
