<h2 class="section-label">Overview</h2>

Rare-event searches live or die by their instrumentation: the reach of a
dark matter or neutrino experiment is set by how cleanly and efficiently it
can register tiny depositions of energy against a background of noise and
radioactivity.

The group develops detector and readout technology for next-generation
experiments, with a particular focus on silicon-photomultiplier-based Photo
Detection Modules (PDMs) and the systems that surround them.

<h2 class="section-label">Current activities</h2>

Current activities include the development and characterisation of Photo
Detection Modules of the type built for the DarkSide-20k neutron veto, and
demonstrator systems that double as public-engagement tools.
