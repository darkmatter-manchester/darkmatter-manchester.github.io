<h2 class="section-label">Overview</h2>

Optically and magnetically levitated mesoscopic particles are among the
most sensitive force and acceleration sensors ever built. By isolating a
nanoscale or microscale object from its environment and reading out its
motion with quantum-limited precision, levitated opto-mechanical systems
can probe forces far too small for conventional detectors.

The group studies how this emerging technology could extend dark matter
searches into mass regimes — and interaction types — inaccessible to
conventional direct-detection experiments.

<h2 class="section-label">Current activities</h2>

Current work focuses on sensitivity projections, signal modelling, and the
physics-case studies that motivate this new class of experiment, alongside
sensor development.
