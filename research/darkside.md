<h2 class="section-label">Overview</h2>

DarkSide-20k is a multi-tonne, dual-phase liquid-argon time projection
chamber under construction at the INFN Laboratori Nazionali del Gran Sasso
(LNGS) in Italy. It is designed to search for weakly interacting massive
particles with an effectively background-free exposure over its lifetime,
using ultra-pure underground argon and silicon-photomultiplier readout.

The group contributes to the construction and physics programme of
DarkSide-20k, and to the reinterpretation of data from its predecessor,
DarkSide-50 — mapping the experiments' sensitivity to spin-independent,
spin-dependent, and effective-field-theory-motivated dark matter models.

<h2 class="section-label">Current activities</h2>

Current activities include sensitivity and physics-reach studies,
contributions to the silicon photodetector modules and the neutron veto,
and analysis development. The group also builds outreach demonstrators
based on the same detector technology.
